%% Appendix F: screenshot gallery
\appx{F}{Interface Screenshots (Supporting Evidence)}
The interface is server-rendered HTML with one stylesheet. It supports keyboard use, colour-independent status badges and a responsive layout; 28 automated checks passed on 17 November 2026 (33 screenshots) (\code{ui-checks.json}): visible focus on every control, no horizontal scrolling at 390 px, a 200-character title that wraps, a decision screen that previews the resulting balance, denied events distinguishable without colour, the throttle behaviour, the second-factor flow, the extend-deadline form and the CSV export. The screenshots were taken on pristine demonstration data, so proposal \#1 appears as \emph{under review}. The very tall full-page captures (the audit timeline and the mobile layouts) are cropped to their top part in this gallery; the complete images are in \code{docs/evidence/screenshots/}. Each caption states what the image demonstrates; the technical evidence is in the sections above.
